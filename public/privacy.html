Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 13, 2026 · Effective for Go Study! (com.gostudy.student)

1. Who We Are

Go Study! (GoStudy) is an educational app by SkyDrake / ScoreTarget, based in Lomé, Togo. We help students track grades, access past exam papers, and plan their studies. Contact: techmira77@gmail.com

2. What Data We Collect

Data you provide: Name (optional), email (if you sign in with Google or email), class level and série, study schedule, grade marks, premium subscription status, and feedback you submit.

Data collected automatically: Device type and OS, app version, session usage, pages viewed, download history (which exam papers/books you download), download quota for free users, search queries in the library, and crash/error logs needed to fix bugs.

We do not collect payment card data. Premium access uses offline codes — no in-app purchases.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • Provide core features: grade calculator, exam library, PDF viewing, schedule reminders
  • Sync your profile and downloads across devices when signed in
  • Enforce free download limits and premium access
  • Send optional study reminders (with your permission)
  • Improve app stability and performance

We do not sell your personal data and do not use it for third-party advertising.

4. Data Storage & Security

Data is stored locally on your device and, when you sign in, synced to Supabase (PostgreSQL on AWS, encrypted in transit via HTTPS/TLS). Downloaded PDFs are stored on your device for offline use.

5. Third-Party Services

  • Supabase — database, authentication, file storage
  • Google Sign-In (optional)
  • Capacitor — mobile app framework
  • Google Play — app distribution (Android)

6. Permissions (Android)

  • Internet — load exam papers, sync account, check updates
  • Storage / media — save and open downloaded PDFs offline
  • Notifications — study reminders (optional, you can disable in Settings)

7. Your Rights & Account Deletion

You can view and edit your profile in the app. To delete your account and cloud data, use Settings → Wipe all data or email techmira77@gmail.com. We will process deletion requests within 30 days.

8. Children's Privacy

Go Study! is designed for secondary and university students (13+). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without parental consent. Parents may contact us to review or delete a child's data.

9. Data Retention

Account data is kept while your account is active. Download quota resets monthly for free users. You may request full deletion at any time.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. Material changes will be noted in the app or on this page. Continued use after changes means you accept the updated policy.
